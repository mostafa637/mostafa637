# SSE3/SSE4.1 JIT64 reference

المجموعة المختارة من فجوات x86asm بعد جرد `jit64.go` هي تعليمات legacy SSE ذات XMM 128-bit: `ADDSUBPD`, `ADDSUBPS`, `HADDPD`, `HADDPS`, `HSUBPD`, `HSUBPS`, `MOVMSKPD`, `MOVMSKPS`, و`PTEST`.

## مصادر Intel/Felix Cloutier

- [ADDSUBPD](https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/addsubpd): legacy `DEST.q0 = DEST.q0 - SRC.q0`, `DEST.q1 = DEST.q1 + SRC.q1`.
- [ADDSUBPS](https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/addsubps): legacy lanes 0..3 alternate subtract/add: `D0=S0-D0`, `D1=D1+S1`, `D2=D2-S2`, `D3=D3+S3`.
- [HADDPD](https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/haddpd): `D0=S1?` وفق operand naming الرسمي: destination low qword receives old destination high+low; destination high receives source high+low.
- [HADDPS](https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/haddps): destination lanes 0/1 receive sums of old destination pairs; lanes 2/3 receive sums of source pairs.
- [HSUBPD](https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/hsubpd): destination low receives old destination low-high; high receives source low-high.
- [HSUBPS](https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/hsubps): destination lanes 0/1 receive old destination pair differences; lanes 2/3 receive source pair differences.
- [MOVMSKPD](https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/movmskpd): extracts sign bits 63 and 127 into destination bits 0 and 1, zero-extends the destination.
- [MOVMSKPS](https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/movmskps): extracts sign bits 31, 63, 95, 127 into destination bits 0..3, zero-extends the destination.
- [PTEST](https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/ptest): `ZF = (SRC & DEST)==0`, `CF = (SRC & ^DEST)==0`, and clears AF/OF/PF/SF.

هذه المرحلة تدعم legacy SSE فقط؛ لا تضيف VEX/AVX ولا تغيّر upper YMM state. وتعليمات arithmetic لا تغيّر EFLAGS، بينما PTEST يحدّث flags المحددة فقط وفق pseudo-code أعلاه.
